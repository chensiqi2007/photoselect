## 1. 簡體中文（中文 (中國) - zh-CN）

### 产品名称 (Product Name)
PhotoSelect

### 说明 / 描述 (Description)
PhotoSelect 是一款专为商业摄影师、婚礼摄影师与影像创作者打造的极速摄影选片与资产管理桌面应用。

不同于传统选片软件冗长繁重的“导入”与“建立目录”流程，PhotoSelect 采用“零等待导入（Zero-Import）”架构，直连您的 SD 存储卡或本地工作目录，实现千张高分辨率照片与主流相机 RAW 文件的毫秒级秒开浏览。

【核心亮点与功能】
• 极速零导入与秒级渲染：即插即选，无需漫长等待将文件复制或导入数据库，即刻开启全速浏览。
• 全格式 RAW 硬核解码：深度集成底层高性能解码引擎，完美支持 Sony (.ARW)、Canon (.CR3)、Nikon (.NEF) 等主流品牌相机的 RAW 原片与全画幅预览。
• 极致流畅的 GPU 暗房体验：基于 WebGL 2.0 硬件加速，实现 100% 细节即时放大、平滑无极缩放与极速平移，精准审查焦点清晰度。
• 专业对比与分组挑片：支持单图审查、双图并排对比（A/B 对比）与多图连拍分组挑片，轻松挑出表情、构图与对焦最佳的一张。
• 快捷打标与星级筛选：全面兼容行业标准的星级评分（1~5星）、色彩标签（红/黄/绿/蓝/紫）与通过/排除标记，支持极速键盘快捷盲打选片。
• 本地优先与绝对隐私：所有照片解码、元数据分析与数据库缓存均严格运行在您的本地设备上，不上传任何照片，保障商业拍摄机密。
• 灵活批处理导出：支持按星级、色彩标签筛选后，一键复制、移动至精修文件夹，或导出对应文件清单，无缝对接 Lightroom 与 Capture One 后期工作流。

## 3. 英語（English (United States) - en-US）

### Product Name
PhotoSelect

### Description
PhotoSelect is a lightning-fast photo culling and image management desktop application engineered for commercial photographers, wedding shooters, and visual creators.

Say goodbye to tedious, time-consuming "Import" processes and bloated catalog files. Built on a revolutionary Zero-Import architecture, PhotoSelect connects directly to your SD memory cards or local storage drives, delivering millisecond-level instant previews across thousands of high-resolution RAW and JPG photos.

KEY FEATURES & HIGHLIGHTS:
• Instant Zero-Import Culling: Plug in your card and start culling immediately. No waiting for files to copy or catalogs to build.
• Native High-Performance RAW Decoding: Powered by a robust native engine, PhotoSelect fully supports RAW formats from major camera brands including Sony (.ARW), Canon (.CR3), Nikon (.NEF), as well as standard formats (JPG, PNG, WebP).
• WebGL 2.0 GPU-Accelerated Darkroom: Inspect focus sharpness with buttery-smooth 100% zoom and effortless pan rendering, even on high-megapixel images (45MP+).
• Side-by-Side Comparison: Compare similar shots side-by-side (A/B view) or inspect burst sequences to find the sharpest focus and best facial expressions.
• Rapid Ratings & Color Labels: Streamline your triage with 1-5 star ratings, 5 color labels, and pick/reject tags. Optimized for blindingly fast single-key keyboard shortcuts.
• Local-First Privacy & Security: All image decoding, EXIF parsing, and local database caching happen 100% on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to the cloud.
• Seamless Post-Processing Workflow: Batch filter, copy, or move your selected favorites into designated folders, or export file lists ready for Lightroom and Capture One.
