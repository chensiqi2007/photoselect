PhotoSelect 隐私权保护政策 (Privacy Policy)

发行组织 (Publisher): PhotoSelect Desktop Open Source Project / URL Time | 生效日期 (Effective Date): 2026-10-18

中文版说明 (Chinese)

1. 核心隐私保护承诺

PhotoSelect 坚守「本机构建、纯离线运行、零数据收集 (Local-First, Offline & Zero Data Collection)」的根本原则。我们深知摄影师作品与客户肖像资产的高度私密性，本软件在系统架构设计上确保您的所有照片、元数据和生物特征绝对留在本地设备。

2. 数据收集与离线处理规范

  • 零个人可识别信息 (PII) 收集：我们不收集、不记录、不上报任何用户的个人身份信息、设备指纹、位置数据或使用偏好。
  • 本机端 RAW 与图片解码：所有图像与视频文件（Sony ARW, Canon CR3, Nikon NEF, Fujifilm RAF 等）的解析、全保真预览流提取、缩略图计算与 SQLite 本地缓存，均在您本地设备的内存与存储介质内完成，绝不上载至外部云端服务器。
  • 纯离线端侧 AI 推理：MediaPipe Face Landmarker 人脸与人眼焦点计算依托 WebAssembly 在本地硬件上独立推论，绝不向网络传输任何面部或眼部生物识别数据。

3. 系统权限声明与合规

本应用程序向操作系统（Windows / Linux）申请的完全受信任文件系统存取权限（Full Trust），仅严格用于让用户选择目录浏览、高速缓存读取与 XMP 侧车写入，绝无任何隐蔽越权操作。

English Version (International)

1. Core Privacy Commitment

PhotoSelect Desktop strictly adheres to the principle of "Local-First, Offline & Zero Data Collection". We recognize the confidential nature of client photographic assets and artistic masterworks. Our software is architected to guarantee that your photos, metadata, and biometric landmarks remain 100% on your local machine.

2. Data Collection and Local Processing

  • Zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect, transmit, store, or profile any personally identifiable information, device IDs, or usage habits.
  • Local RAW Processing: All RAW decoding (Sony ARW, Canon CR3, Nikon NEF, Fujifilm RAF, Adobe DNG) and thumbnail generation take place strictly within your local CPU/GPU and SSD cache.
  • Offline On-Device AI: MediaPipe 468-point facial mesh and eye sharpness evaluation run entirely via offline WebAssembly models without network connections.

3. System Permissions

The file system access requested by PhotoSelect on Windows and Linux is used solely to read user-selected photography folders and write back standard .xmp sidecar metadata.
