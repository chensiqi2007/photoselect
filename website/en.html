ARCHITECTURAL EXCELLENCE

6 Pillars of Next-Gen Culling Speed

Say goodbye to the "wait 20 minutes to import, lag for an hour" headache. PhotoSelect re-architects photographic workflows from the ground up.

⚡ Zero-Import Architecture

Zero-Import · Instant Access from Camera Cards

Ditch the agonizing waiting times of catalog ingestion, file copying, and monolithic thumbnail cache databases. Connect your SD or CFexpress card reader, pick a folder, and begin culling thousands of 50MP+ RAW files in milliseconds.

  • Instant Folder Browsing: 0-second launch overhead, stream files on-demand directly
  • Virtual Viewport & 2-Tier LRU Cache: Stable <80MB idle memory even with 10,000+ images
🔗 Smart Pairing

Seamless RAW + JPG Binding

Automatically pairs same-name in-camera JPEGs and factory RAWs (ARW, CR3, NEF, RAF, DNG) into unified asset cards. Cull at lightning speed on the JPG, while ratings and exports link automatically to the RAW original.

  • Zero duplicate passes through redundant files
  • Streaming export copies only chosen RAW files
🎯 GPU Shader Technology

WebGL 2.0 Fragment Shader Focus Peaking

No need to click and zoom into every single shot. WebGL 2.0 shader technology computes edge contrast in real-time on your GPU, outlining in-focus zones with neon cyan and green contours. Instant clarity on eyelashes vs. nose tips.

  • Toggle instantly with the F key without rendering lag
  • Spacebar or double-click for instant 1:1 true-pixel zoom
👁️ 468-Point Facial Mesh

Narrative Select-Style 100% Eye Inspection

Integrated offline MediaPipe Face Landmarker model detects every subject face in the frame. Crops left and right eyes at 100% true physical pixels, computing sharpness percentages and blink alerts simultaneously.

  • 100% offline inference — no biometric data ever leaves your device
  • Click the eye micro-card to snap and center the main viewport instantly
⚡ 3D Folded Stacks

SubSecTime Burst Cluster & Best Shot Selection

Extracts EXIF sub-second and microsecond timestamps to group 20–120 fps burst sequences accurately. In Group Folded View, bursts collapse into 3D stacked cards, highlighting the algorithm-recommended Best Shot with a single click to Pick Winner & Reject Others.

  • One-Click Cull: Pick best frame and batch-reject transitions
  • 64-bit dHash 0.5ms perceptual fingerprint eliminates identical redundant bursts
⚖️ Dual View Synchronization

Synchronized Compare View & Full Adobe LrC XMP Sync

Side-by-side [A] vs [B] split comparison with synchronized panning and zoom. Press S to swap positions. Full bidirectional compatibility with Adobe Lightroom Classic .xmp sidecar files and hierarchical taxonomy trees.

  • Write back sidecars in-place without exporting for zero workflow friction
  • Supports hierarchical taxonomy (e.g. Animals|Birds|Red-billed Leiothrix)

Frequently Asked Questions

Key answers regarding zero-import, privacy, XMP workflows, and commercial licensing.

Traditional tools like Lightroom Classic require an upfront import phase: copying files, generating massive catalog databases, and compiling Smart Previews. If you come back from a wedding with 3,000 photos, you typically wait 20 minutes before inspecting the first shot. PhotoSelect reads directly from the file system like Photo Mechanic. Plug in your card and start culling immediately.
PhotoSelect detects matching pairs (e.g., DSC08492.ARW and DSC08492.JPG) and unifies them into a single asset card. You preview the lightweight, lightning-fast JPG in the foreground, while your star ratings, flags (Pick/Reject), and export commands apply automatically to the underlying RAW file in the background.
100% NO. Everything runs locally and offline. PhotoSelect bundles Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker model compiled to WebAssembly. All 468 facial landmark coordinates, 100% eye crops, and sharpness metrics are evaluated entirely in your local RAM/GPU. No client portrait photos or biometric data ever touch an external server.
PhotoSelect adheres to international .xmp sidecar standards. When culling, star ratings (0-5), flags (Pick/Reject), color labels, and hierarchical keywords are saved into sidecars beside your RAWs. When you import that folder into Lightroom Classic, every rating and flag appears instantly, preserving your existing retouching workflow.
